The course is aimed at graduate students and other researchers. It assumes you have prior experience working with R. These requirements can be fulfilled by completing a Software Carpentry R workshop, which will be hosted by the Charité Scientific Computing Team on Sep 29-30 and Oct 6-7 2026 (Software Carpentry Workshop at the Charité). You can sign up here to the Software Carpentry R workshop.

Should this prerequisite workshop reach capacity or you simply cannot attend it, we strongly encourage you to go through the Software Carpentry R workshop by yourself before attending this one.
